Roadster — Thruster Launch & Barrel Rolls

[h1]Launch. Grip. Roll.[/h1]
Give your next drive a little more ambition. Roadster adds a sharp, white sports car with switchable motor and nitrogen-thruster launches, fan-assisted downforce and a thruster-powered barrel roll.

[h2]Behind the wheel[/h2]
[list]
[*]Choose your launch: press N to switch between motors and nitrogen thrusters.
[*]Launch from a standstill with the wheels near straight and full throttle.
[*]Fan downforce adds grip while the car is on the road.
[*]Press J for a barrel roll. Steer to choose the roll direction; straight ahead defaults to a right roll.
[*]Rear and downward thruster plumes accompany the launch and stunt.
[/list]

[h2]Finding the Roadster[/h2]
The current build adds the car to General US Trucks at 1 7th Avenue, priced at $250,000.

[h2]Preview build — version 0.0.2[/h2]
This is an experimental preview. Handling, visuals and compatibility are still being checked. The cover is promotional artwork based on the mod's model; it is not an in-game screenshot.

Use a separate test save and keep a backup before enabling the mod. Removing the mod loses Roadsters held in affected saves.

[h2]Controls[/h2]
N — switch launch mode
J — barrel roll
Normal driving controls — steer, accelerate and brake

[h2]Feedback[/h2]
When reporting a problem, include your game version, operating system, active mods and what happened immediately before the issue. Relevant [Mod:Roadster] log lines are useful too.
