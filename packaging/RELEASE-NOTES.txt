Roadster 0.0.3 — released

Headlights, tail lights and amber marker lights now follow the car's normal
light state instead of glowing constantly. Night/rain switching and leaving
the car use vanilla behaviour. Brake lights brighten when braking.

Dealer colour choices now repaint the body and rear wing, while glass,
wheels and trim keep their own colours.

Includes 0.0.2's faster stunt landings and straight-run launch assistance.

Barrel roll moves from J to Backslash (\), leaving J free for HART. Self Driving,
and requires speed above 2 m/s (about 4.5 mph). Refs #2.

Passed the PC rig test on 1 Nov 2026: motor launch (0-60 mph in about 1.3 s),
nitrogen launch timing, a clean left barrel roll, and the Backslash roll
binding with J free for HART. Self Driving.
