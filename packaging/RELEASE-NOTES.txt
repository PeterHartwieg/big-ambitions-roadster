Roadster 0.0.2 — release candidate, 23 October 2026

Faster landings bring the car back down sooner after a stunt. Launch assistance
now engages only during straight runs, keeping it out of turns.

Includes the custom Roadster model, switchable motor/nitrogen launches,
grounded fan downforce, directional barrel roll and thruster plumes.

The SDK build and package checksum checks are offline verification. Final
in-game acceptance of the landing and launch changes remains pending; see CHECKS.md.
