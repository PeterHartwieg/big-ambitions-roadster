Roadster 0.0.3 — release candidate, 23 October 2026

Headlights, tail lights and amber marker lights now follow the car's normal
light state instead of glowing constantly. Night/rain switching and leaving
the car use vanilla behaviour. Brake lights brighten when braking.

Dealer colour choices now repaint the body and rear wing, while glass,
wheels and trim keep their own colours.

Includes 0.0.2's faster stunt landings and straight-run launch assistance.

Offline Unity checks exercise the native colour and light methods against
local game assemblies. The SDK builds Windows and Mac asset bundles, and
the release packager verifies its checksums and ZIP contents. These checks
do not establish in-game appearance, dealer/purchase/save/reload behaviour
or gameplay acceptance on either platform. See CHECKS.md for Peter's checks.
