# Reference review and evidence

Front is Blender −Y; the driver's side is +X. The wheel hubs and spoiler hinge retain the specified coordinates. All files generated by this run are inside this output folder. Pass 3 was opened as the starting scene and was not saved over.

The four comparison rows are front, elevated straight rear, stage rear three-quarter, and driving side. The model is on the left. Each overlay blends the render and photograph at 50%, with the same image rectangle and camera; the car is not separately stretched to fit. Camera parameters are in `camera-match.json`. The work scene contains camera backgrounds and pixel-traced outline/crease guides. Delivered camera backgrounds are removed, and `Photo_Trace_Guides` is excluded from the view layer and renders.

## Round 1 — blockout

Evidence: `renders/history/01-blockout-*.png`, `renders/history/compare-1-before-regeneration.png`, `renders/compare-1b.png`, and the corresponding `overlay-*-1.png` / `overlay-*-1b.png`.

- **Front:** the source wheel-well shields projected above the hood and looked like vents. The inner fender surfaces and cowl did not form a clean continuous quilt. Replaced the shell, cut those shields below the hood, removed the grille, and gave the intake a smooth dark band. Traced the triangular lamp boundaries and made the fender quilt stop at them.
- **Rear:** the shoulders were too flat, the closed wing read as a separate bronze plate, and the window was too broad at its foot. Built sloping shoulders, changed the wing's top to repaintable body paint, and narrowed the rear window into a symmetric trapezoid.
- **Rear three-quarter:** the fascia was a flat rectangle and the diffuser read as an exposed attachment. Rolled the fascia under, recessed the fan assemblies and nozzle row, and shortened the diffuser strakes.
- **Side:** the hood had a step, the glass started too far aft, the rear quarter was too square, and the black area was too large. Brought the central glass forward, blended the cowl into the windscreen, lowered and sloped the outer shoulders, and revised the black band. Removed the mirror stalks; only tiny flush lens details remain.

## Round 2 — surfaces and details

Evidence: `renders/history/02-detail-*.png`, `renders/history/compare-2-before-regeneration.png`, `renders/compare-3.png` through `compare-6.png`, and their overlays.

- **Front:** the white fender crowns still looked broad and the corners were too square in plan. Narrowed their top edges, leaned the upper outer faces inward, aligned a sharp inner crease above the steering envelope, and swept the bumper corners behind the central nose. The 0.8 m-high wheel-travel volumes were checked with mesh/box intersection tests after these changes.
- **Rear:** coincident wing/deck surfaces caused dark slashes. Removed the skin beneath the closed blade and built an actual recessed pocket and surrounding painted borders. Folded the bronze supports below it. A bright view through the nozzle row was caught in the textured close-up and closed with a black backing panel.
- **Rear three-quarter:** the initial camera was too high. Refitted the camera to the plate/light bar and wheel landmarks, then compared again. Darkened the fans, kept the four bronze rings bright, moved the box inward, and lifted the outer bumper's underside so it no longer hangs to the floor behind the tyres.
- **Side:** the black band still swallowed the white panel in front of the rear wheel. Lowered it there, retained the steep aft rise, and let the wheel opening provide the black arch silhouette. The nose is now swept, and the roof eases into broad shoulders rather than a high rectangular quarter.
- **Colour and shading:** a large rear studio strip reflected as a white patch in the windscreen. Moved that strip sideways, retained the dark warm glass, and made the thin canopy outline painted white. The source wheels, rim machining, brake parts and turbine/nozzle designs were retained.

## Round 3 — textured review

Evidence: `renders/compare-final.png`, `renders/overlay-*-final.png`, individual final views, and `renders/review-sheet.png`.

- Repacked a unique atlas shared by the six visible meshes and copied it to UV2. Baked geometric AO, with a small margin to protect neighbouring islands and short rays for local contact shading. The final material consumes base colour, metallic, smoothness, normal and AO. All required emission colours and separate channel masks are present. The ten jet throats stay dark in base colour; Blender's studio material shows the jets idle while retaining their pale-blue game emission map.
- Checked the fitted views again after texturing, fixed the bright diffuser backing and reviewed the rear corner shading. The striped artifact was traced to opposite triangulation diagonals on thin non-planar front/back skins. Explicit matching diagonals removed it, and the full regeneration pipeline was then retested. The wing remains closed; photo 03 shows it raised, so that difference is intentional.
- The collision hull uses a conservative supporting-plane envelope of the actual body and wheel vertices, `bmesh.ops.convex_hull`, planar facet repair and triangulation. Reducing the support-plane set removed unstable thin facets; its measured convexity error is about 1.3 micrometres.

## Remaining likeness limits

The result is not an exact photographic reconstruction. In particular, the front crowns remain fuller and more angular than the reference blades; the fixed rectangular wheel-travel volumes constrain how far their outer faces can lean inward below z = 0.80 m. The side glass foot, roof curvature and rear shoulder transitions remain approximations, and a few-centimetre overlay was not achieved along every part of the side outline. The diffuser housing is more regular than the photographed assembly. Lighting, lens perspective, subtle pearl response, lamp optics and fine fan mounting details differ from the reveal images.

The normal map is flat, as permitted; visible detail is modelled. Doors are closed static geometry with inset channels, not a door rig. There is no text or branding. Unity import appearance, steering/suspension in the game and driving behaviour are **pending**; this task does not install the asset into the game.
