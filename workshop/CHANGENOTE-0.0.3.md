# RDSTR 0.0.3

- Fixed headlights, tail lights and amber marker lights glowing all the time. They follow the car's normal light state, including automatic night/rain lighting and switching off when you leave the car. Brake lights brighten when braking.
- Fixed dealer colour choices not repainting the body and rear wing. Glass, wheels and trim keep their own colours.
- Moved barrel roll from J to Backslash (`\`) to avoid the HART Self Driving key clash; rolls now require speed above 2 m/s (about 4.5 mph). Refs #2.
